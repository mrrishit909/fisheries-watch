Fisheries Watch

An invented sea with a marine reserve, 110 fishing boats, four reefer carriers and twelve days of AIS as it was really received. Which silences are on purpose, which radar targets have no AIS and who they could be, which carriers met someone at sea, and which boats to look at first, every finding set against the simulation truth. …
The region, the fleet, its tracks, the AIS reception, the radar passes and who broke the rules are generated by a simulator in this repository; no real vessel, MMSI, registry or satellite data is used. The models were trained on past cases from other simulated fortnights. The truth is shown only to judge the detectors, which never see it. No outside model or service is called.
